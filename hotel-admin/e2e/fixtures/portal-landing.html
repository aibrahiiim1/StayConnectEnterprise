How we use your data

This Wi-Fi is provided by its operator, which is responsible for your personal data on it. To give you internet access and keep the network secure, it records your device’s network addresses, when you connect and how much data you use, and what you sign in with. These records are deleted or made anonymous after a set time. Ask the staff here for the full privacy notice, or to see, correct or delete your data.

Help with signing in

  • RoomEnter your room number, then the detail asked for below it, exactly as it appears on your reservation.
  • Post-stayAlready checked out? Enter the PIN you were given at checkout to reconnect.
  • VoucherType the code exactly as it is printed on your voucher, then tap Login.
  • Personal accountEnter the username and password you were given. If a voucher field is showing, switch on “Use Personal Account” first.
  • EmailSign in with your email address and password. New here? Tap “Create an account”, then open the link in the email we send you to verify the address. Forgot your password? Tap “Forgot password?” for a reset link.
  • PhoneEnter your phone number with the country code and tap Send code, then type the 6-digit code from the text message.
  • WhatsAppEnter your WhatsApp phone number with the country code and tap Send code, then type the 6-digit code from the WhatsApp message.
  • SocialYou will be redirected to the provider, then back here.
